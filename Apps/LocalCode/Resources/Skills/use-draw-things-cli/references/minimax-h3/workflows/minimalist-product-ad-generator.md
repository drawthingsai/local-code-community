# Minimalist product ad

Use for a restrained product film built around clear form, tactile detail, and
short copy. Use the shared [production workflow](../production.md) for assets and
assembly, and [H3 prompting](../prompting.md) for generation.

## Build the visual argument

Identify the exact product and variant, its recognizable silhouette, materials,
color, and the one idea the film should communicate. Preserve the supplied product's
identity. A style change must not recolor a real product or redesign its controls.
Turn an actual product property into a visible action rather than inventing a
mechanism or specification to make the ad more exciting.

Choose a coherent treatment: bright white studio, dark rim-lit studio, a field of
the brand's color, or restrained lifestyle context. Let lighting reveal the product's
geometry. Avoid filling the composition with unrelated decorations or generic
technology effects.

For a complete production, prepare three independent anchors as needed:

- Hero: a clear overall product view with breathing room.
- Detail: a meaningful material, functional part, or interaction.
- Copy: a composition that gives the product and exact words a readable hierarchy.

Keep the anchors as separate full-frame assets and use the suitable one for each
clip. Choose shot count from the narrative and duration. Use a clean hero frame
when the product's full silhouette needs to be established before motion.

## Design beats and copy together

Write the sequence as reveal, development, payoff, and a readable ending, with
specific actions and camera behavior. A product movement should start, travel,
and settle plausibly; surfaces, shadows, and reflections should react with it.
Choose cuts and transitions that reveal product information.

Favor short, three-to-five-word lines, restrained sans-serif typography, and at most
two text colors: a high-contrast base and a real product/brand accent. Preserve the
user's language and exact copy. Keep one line active at a time, composed into the image
rather than placed automatically in a subtitle strip. Stagger entrances subtly
while keeping the line readable; avoid large late movements just before a cut.

Time the reveal, action, and copy hold to the actual generated duration. Describe
music and product sound in H3's audio sections. Add a supplied master track during
final editing when the request calls for that exact recording.

Inspect silhouette, color, labels, contact/shadow behavior, copy readability, and
the final hold. Correct the failing shot while preserving the established product
and visual treatment.
